Privacy policy

TrainGuard · Last updated 18 November 2026

Your tickets stay on your phone. TrainGuard has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data.

Your tickets

The ticket SMS or email text you paste is read on your phone and saved only in TrainGuard's storage on that phone. It's never uploaded. Removing a trip, uninstalling the app or clearing the website's data deletes it.

Your location

TrainGuard uses your location only when you turn on a feature that needs it:

  • Where is my train uses your location while the app is open, to estimate where your train is and whether it's running late.
  • GPS wake-up, in the iPhone app, uses it to ring before your stop, including when the app is closed.

Your location is worked out and used on your phone. It's never sent to us or anyone else.

Alarms, notifications and your calendar

Wake-up alarms, notifications and the journey countdown are set up on your phone. When you tap Add to calendar, TrainGuard adds the event on your phone or opens your calendar app with it filled in. It doesn't read your calendar.

Clipboard and sharing

TrainGuard reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste from clipboard, and copies to it only when you tap a copy button or open an official page. A shared trip link carries the trip in the part after the #, which browsers don't send to any server. Your PNR is left out unless you choose to include it.

Official websites

The live status and PNR status buttons open Indian Railways websites. Their own privacy policies apply there.

The website

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, its servers may log technical details such as IP addresses to deliver the site. TrainGuard adds no analytics or tracking of its own.

Children

TrainGuard isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Not an official app

TrainGuard is independent. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Indian Railways, CRIS or IRCTC. Train times come from a community-maintained timetable of Indian Railways and may be out of date, so your ticket and the station displays always come first.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
